Why is Krishnagiri Called the Mango Capital of India?

Krishnagiri is widely known as the "Mango Capital of India" — and it's a title backed by real numbers, not just marketing. The district produces roughly 300,000 tonnes of mangoes every year, making it the leading mango-producing district in Tamil Nadu and one of the biggest mango-growing regions in the entire country. Krishnagiri alone accounts for more than 37% of Tamil Nadu's total mango production!

Mango Production by the Numbers

  • 🥭 ~300,000 tonnes of mangoes produced annually
  • 🥭 Over 37% of Tamil Nadu's total mango production comes from Krishnagiri
  • 🥭 Leading mango-producing district in Tamil Nadu
  • 🥭 Popular varieties grown include Thothapuri, Alphonso (Banganapalli), and Neelam

The Mango Processing Industry

Mangoes from Krishnagiri don't just get sold fresh — a significant processing industry has grown around them. Roughly 20% of varieties like Thothapuri are processed into mango pulp, and large quantities are also turned into mango juice each year. This processing industry supports thousands of local jobs, from farming to factory work.

Krishnagiri's Mango Export Zone

Recognizing the scale of mango production in the district, a large-scale mango export zone has been approved for Krishnagiri, aiming to help local farmers and processors reach international markets more directly. This reflects just how central mango farming is to the district's economy.
