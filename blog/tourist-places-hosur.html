Why Visit Hosur?

Hosur is a fast-growing town in Krishnagiri district, Tamil Nadu, located just about 40 km from Bangalore. Known for its pleasant year-round climate, Hosur is often called the "Little England of India"! While it's famous as an industrial and automobile hub, Hosur also has beautiful temples, peaceful lakes, and scenic hills worth exploring — making it a great day trip whether you're coming from Bangalore or Krishnagiri.

1. Malai Kovil (Chandra Choodeshwarar Temple)

Perched on a hill near Hosur, Malai Kovil — home to the Arulmigu Sri Chandra Choodeshwarar Temple — is one of Hosur's most famous landmarks, dedicated to Lord Shiva. It offers peaceful panoramic views over the surrounding countryside and is especially crowded on Mondays and weekends when devotees visit from across the region, so plan accordingly if you'd prefer a quieter visit.

2. Kelavarapalli Dam

Kelavarapalli Dam (also known as Kelavarapalli Reservoir) is a peaceful spot popular for picnics, boating, and birdwatching. Surrounded by greenery, it's a relaxing escape from Hosur's industrial hustle.

3. Walkers Path and Children's Park

Located in central Hosur, this well-maintained park is ideal for families, with safe walking trails, a children's play area, and plenty of green space for a relaxed evening out.

4. Muthanallur Lake (Near Hosur, Karnataka)

Spread across roughly 600 acres, Muthanallur Lake is just across the border in Karnataka, a short drive from Hosur. It's known for its clear waters and the large number of birds that visit during peak season — a nice stop for nature lovers and birdwatchers if you're up for a short trip out of town.

5. Hogenakal Falls (Day Trip from Hosur)

Known as the "Niagara of India," Hogenakal Falls is about 80 km from Hosur and makes for a great day trip if you have extra time. It's actually one of the top attractions in the wider Krishnagiri district — check out our complete Krishnagiri tourist places guide for more on getting there.

Getting to Hosur

Hosur is well connected by road:

  • 🚗 From Bangalore: ~40 km, about 1 hour via Hosur Road (NH 48)
  • 🚗 From Krishnagiri town: ~50 km, about 1 hour via NH 44

Regular buses run between Hosur, Bangalore, and Krishnagiri, making it an easy day trip from either city.

Frequently Asked Questions About Tourist Places in Hosur

1. What are the best tourist places in Hosur?

The best tourist places in Hosur include Malai Kovil (Chandra Choodeshwarar Temple), Kelavarapalli Dam, and Walkers Path and Children's Park. Muthanallur Lake (just across the border in Karnataka) and Hogenakal Falls also make great day trips from Hosur!

2. How far is Hosur from Bangalore?

Hosur is approximately 40 km from Bangalore, taking about 1 hour by road via Hosur Road (NH 48).

3. How far is Hosur from Krishnagiri?

Hosur is approximately 50 km from Krishnagiri town, taking about 1 hour by road via NH 44.

4. Why is Hosur called "Little England of India"?

Hosur earned this nickname because of its pleasant, cool climate throughout the year compared to much of Tamil Nadu, along with its green, tree-lined landscape.

5. Is Hosur worth visiting as a day trip?

Yes! Hosur is an easy day trip from both Bangalore and Krishnagiri, with a good mix of temples, lakes, and nature spots, plus the option to continue on to Hogenakal Falls if you have extra time.
