Why is Hosur Known as an Industrial Hub?

Hosur sits right on the border of Tamil Nadu and Karnataka, along NH-44, just about 40 km from Bangalore's Electronic City. This strategic location — combined with Tamil Nadu's industry-friendly policies and lower operational costs compared to Bangalore — has turned Hosur into one of South India's most important manufacturing centers over the past few decades. What started as an agricultural region has grown into a major hub for automobiles, electronics, and precision engineering.

SIPCOT Industrial Complex

The backbone of Hosur's industrial growth is the SIPCOT (State Industries Promotion Corporation of Tamil Nadu) Industrial Complex, one of the largest industrial parks in the country. It has grown significantly since its original development, and SIPCOT is currently developing a new 3,000+ acre industrial park to meet rising demand — including a dedicated Future Mobility Park focused on electric vehicle manufacturing and green technology.

Major Companies in Hosur

Hosur is home to some of India's biggest industrial names, including:

  • TVS Motor Company — two and three-wheeler manufacturing
  • Ashok Leyland — commercial vehicles and buses
  • Daimler India Commercial Vehicles
  • Carborundum Universal — abrasives and industrial ceramics
  • TTK Prestige — home appliances and cookware
  • Reckitt Benckiser — consumer goods manufacturing
  • LUK India (Schaeffler Group) — automotive clutch systems

Beyond these major players, Hosur is supported by an ecosystem of roughly 3,000 small and medium enterprises (MSMEs) providing components, fabrication, and logistics services.

Hosur — India's Emerging EV Valley

In recent years, Hosur has increasingly been called India's "EV Valley," with electric vehicle manufacturers like Ather Energy and Simple Energy setting up operations here, alongside the traditional automobile giants. The upcoming Future Mobility Park is expected to further strengthen Hosur's position in India's electric vehicle and green technology manufacturing sector.

Why Hosur's Location Works So Well

  • 🚗 Just ~40 km from Bangalore's Electronic City via NH-44
  • ✈️ Convenient access to Bangalore International Airport for exports
  • 🛣️ Positioned along the Bangalore-Chennai industrial corridor
  • 💰 Lower real estate and operational costs than Bangalore, with Tamil Nadu's industry-friendly policies

Frequently Asked Questions About Hosur's Industrial Sector

1. Why is Hosur famous for industries?

Hosur is famous for its automobile and manufacturing industries, largely due to the SIPCOT Industrial Complex and its strategic location near Bangalore, along with Tamil Nadu's supportive industrial policies.

2. What companies are located in Hosur?

Major companies in Hosur include TVS Motor Company, Ashok Leyland, Daimler India Commercial Vehicles, Carborundum Universal, TTK Prestige, and Reckitt Benckiser, among thousands of smaller manufacturing enterprises.

3. Why is Hosur called India's EV Valley?

Hosur has attracted several electric vehicle manufacturers, including Ather Energy and Simple Energy, and is developing a dedicated Future Mobility Park for EV and green technology manufacturing.

4. How far is Hosur from Bangalore?

Hosur is approximately 40 km from Bangalore's Electronic City, making it an easy commute for businesses connected to Bangalore's economy.
